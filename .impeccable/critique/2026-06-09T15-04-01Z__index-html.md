## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment found 6/8 AI-slop signals: cream/paper bg in AI-cream band, numbered section markers (01-04), border-left pullquotes, repetitive accent-rules (6 instances), aphoristic copy cadence, 13 em dashes. Deterministic scan confirmed em-dash-overuse and numbered-section-markers. No false positives.

## What's Working

1. Two Doors IA: splitting by care-leaver journey stage is empathetic, specific, and non-generic.
2. Quick-exit system architecture: four access points plus keyboard, with history replacement.
3. Founder section earns its space: lived experience without sentimentality.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Mobile exit button has no CSS fallback (safety-critical)
On mobile (<900px), header exit is hidden via CSS. Floating exit requires JS + 400px scroll. No visible exit in first viewport without JS. Hero "leave quickly" link is 0.74rem at 66% opacity. Fix: CSS-only fixed exit, single-Esc trigger.

### [P1] Page weight hostile to target connection
Three full-res images (up to 1900x1200), Google Fonts CDN, background-attachment: fixed, no srcset. Could exceed 2MB / 30+ seconds on 3G in Kenya. Fix: self-host subset fonts, responsive images, remove fixed attachment on mobile.

### [P2] AI slop signals undermine bespoke brand promise
Numbered pillars, 6 identical accent-rules, cream+noise bg, border-left pullquotes, 13 em dashes. Fix: remove numbered markers, vary accent-rules, replace border-left pullquotes, replace em dashes.

### [P2] Emotional monotony — care leaver voice quieter than founder's
Three peaks use same rhetorical device. Testimonial gets less visual drama than founder section. Fix: dedicated testimonial treatment, vary rhetorical rhythm.

### [P3] Browser tab title exposes visit on shared devices
"Second Chances -- Supporting Care Leavers" is identifiable. Fix: neutral title or dynamic swap.
